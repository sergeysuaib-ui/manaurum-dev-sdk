Why clients leave after the first month, and the one reason we can fix

12 March 2026Customers4 min read

We read forty cancellation notes side by side. Most reasons were out of our hands. One was not, and it costs a phone call.

Every cancellation note since September went into one table: when the customer joined, how many orders they placed, what they wrote when they left. Forty of them, and three patterns.

Price and fit

Half left because the product was not for them, or not at that price. That is a question for the landing page, not for the first month, and the pricing notes cover it.

Nobody asked how the first two weeks went. I assumed nobody would notice if I stopped. one of the forty

Silence in week two

The rest had one thing in common: no contact between the welcome message and the first invoice. The customers who got a call in week two stayed at three times the rate.

  • Call on day ten, not day thirty.
  • Ask one question: what almost made you stop?
  • Write the answer down where the next person will see it.

That is the whole change. It is in the call script from February.
